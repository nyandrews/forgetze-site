Built to remember, private by design

Everything you need to keep the people in your life straight, and nothing you do not.

Search anything you remember

A note, a street, a child's name, a car. Forgetze finds the person and names the detail that matched, so you can trust the result.

Say it instead of typing it

Tap the microphone and speak what you remember. Add the Forgetze button to Control Center and one tap opens the app listening, from any screen.

The whole picture

Photos, phone numbers, email addresses, birthdays, a spouse, children, addresses and social links. The details that make a person memorable.

Easy to start

Import from Apple Contacts in one tap, then add the details it never held. You can try the app first with a set of sample people.

Private, with no account

No account and no ads. Your people stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, which only you can read. Forgetze never sees them. Read the privacy policy.

On every device

iPhone and iPad, in light or dark mode, in the color you choose. Your people sync through your own iCloud, so a new phone has them waiting.

More features

  • Birthday reminders. Never miss one for the people you want to remember.
  • Back up to a file. Save your library and restore it whenever you need it.
  • Works offline. Search needs no internet connection.
  • Your app color. Pick a theme color, in light or dark mode.
  • Search that stays with you. Works the same in dark mode and on iPad.
  • Anonymous counts you control. Optional feature counts, never your people or searches. Turn them off in Settings.
