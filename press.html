About the app

You remember the face, the conversation, the kids' names. Everything except their name. Forgetze keeps the small details that make a person memorable, then finds them from any one of those details. Type or say a clue such as "drives a Tesla" or "met at the hiking club". Forgetze finds the person and names the detail that matched.

Each person can hold photos, phone numbers, email addresses, birthdays, a spouse, children, addresses and social links. You can import from Apple Contacts in one tap, then add the details it never held. The app also has birthday reminders, and it can back up your library to a file. Search needs no internet connection.

There is no account to make and there are no ads. Your people stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, which only you can read. Forgetze never sees them.
